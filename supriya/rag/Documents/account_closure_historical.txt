Entity: customer, account
Historical Business Knowledge: Customer Account Closure

When a customer account is closed, the account enters a closed state and must no longer be treated as an active account.

Account closure is a business event that can affect other products and activities associated with the customer account.

A customer account may have cards associated with it. Cards associated with a closed account must not continue to operate as active cards for transactions against the closed account.

A customer account may have deposits associated with it. Deposit products associated with a closed account require appropriate closure or settlement handling according to the applicable deposit business process.

A customer account may have transaction history associated with it. Historical transactions remain part of the account history after account closure and must remain distinguishable from new transaction activity.

Account closure must not remove historical transaction records.

The account closure process should therefore be considered complete only when the account status reflects closure and the account is no longer treated as active.

The expected handling of associated cards and deposits may depend on the applicable product and operational process.
