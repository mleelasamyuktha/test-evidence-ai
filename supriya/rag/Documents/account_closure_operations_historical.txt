Entity: customer, account, card, deposit, transaction
Business Area: Customer Account Operations
Scenario: End-to-End Customer Account Closure
Business Context:
Customer account closure is an end-to-end business process. Closing an account may require coordinated handling of products and activities associated with the account, including cards, deposits, and transaction processing.
Business Requirement:
The account closure process must ensure that the customer account and its related products are left in a consistent state after closure.
Business Rules:
valid customer is when customer id always exists.
The customer account must reach the INACTIVE state only after the required account closure activities have been completed.
All cards associated with the account must be prevented from being used for new transactions after the account is closed.
New transactions must not be accepted for an inactive account.
Previously completed transactions must remain available as historical information.
Deposits associated with the account must be handled according to the applicable closure process.
Historical relationships between the customer account and its associated cards, deposits, and transactions must remain available.
The closure process must not unintentionally modify historical transaction or deposit information.
If the closure process cannot complete a required activity, the account must not be represented as successfully closed.
Historical Acceptance Criteria:
A successfully completed closure leaves the customer account in the INACTIVE state.
Associated cards cannot be used for new transactions after closure.
New transactions cannot be created for an inactive account.
Previously completed transactions remain available.
Required deposit-related activities are completed as part of the account closure process.
Historical relationships between the account and its related products remain available.
Historical financial records are not unintentionally removed or modified during closure.
An unsuccessful closure process does not result in the account being incorrectly represented as successfully closed.
