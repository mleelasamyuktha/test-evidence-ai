Entity: deposit

Historical Business Knowledge: Deposit Processing

Deposits are associated with customer accounts.

A customer account belongs to a customer.

A deposit should belong to the same customer who owns the associated account.

Deposit information should remain consistent with the associated account and customer relationship.

When a customer account is closed, deposits associated with the account may require additional processing before the account closure process can be considered fully complete.

Deposit processing may include settlement, closure, transfer, or other product-specific handling.

The expected treatment depends on the type and state of the deposit product.

Deposit records and their historical information should be retained for operational and audit purposes.
