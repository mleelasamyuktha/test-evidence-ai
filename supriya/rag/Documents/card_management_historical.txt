Entity: card

Historical Business Knowledge: Card Management

Cards are associated with customer accounts.

A card associated with an account must not continue to be treated as an active payment instrument when the associated account has been closed.

Account closure may therefore require related card handling.

The card management process should distinguish between cards associated with active accounts and cards associated with closed accounts.

Card history should be retained for audit and operational purposes.

The exact final card status following account closure depends on the applicable card management process and should not be assumed when the current requirement does not explicitly define it.
